Movement reminders at work for iPhone

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I turn on work reminders?
Open the Settings tab, turn on Work reminders, choose your workdays, work periods, and reminder interval, tap Save, and allow iOS notifications.
How do I switch or exclude exercises?
Tap the animated demonstration on the Break tab to switch to another exercise, or open the Exercises tab to browse all 36 bodyweight movements and exclude any you prefer not to see.
How do time zones and holidays work?
Reminders follow your device's local time zone automatically. In Settings → Holidays, you can pause reminders on observed U.S. federal holidays, mainland China holidays, or select None.
Why didn't I receive a reminder?
Check iPhone notification permissions in iOS Settings, verify the Scheduled through date at the bottom of the app's Settings tab, and confirm that your 30-day free trial or monthly subscription is active.
How do the 30-day free trial and subscription work?
Automatic work reminders are free to use for 30 days with no subscription required. After 30 days, exercise previews, the manual timer, and break history remain free, and a monthly auto-renewable subscription keeps automatic work reminders running. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, or tap Restore Purchases on the app's Subscription screen when switching devices.

Safety Note

The exercises in Move Break are provided for general movement breaks only. Move within your comfort range and stop immediately if you feel pain or discomfort.
