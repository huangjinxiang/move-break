Privacy Policy

Move Break · Last updated November 2026

Move Break reminds you to take breaks during the work hours you set and shows bodyweight exercises.

The app stores your workdays, work periods, reminder interval, activity duration, holiday calendar preference, excluded exercises, trial start date, and break history locally on your device. It uses this information to schedule reminders, suggest exercises, and show your history. The app does not require an account or send this information to the developer or third parties. It does not use ads or analytics services.

When you enable reminders, the app asks for notification permission. You can turn off work reminders in the app or disable notifications in iPhone Settings. Turning off reminders leaves existing break history on your device.

Automatic work reminders are free to use for 30 days without a subscription. After the 30-day free trial, optional monthly subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details or Apple Account billing information.

To remove the settings and history stored on your device, choose Delete App in iOS. If you contact support at jackhuang.nj@outlook.com, the information you provide is used only to respond to your request and is deleted when no longer needed.
